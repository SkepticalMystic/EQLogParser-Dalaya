Dalaya vs Upstream

What changed in this fork and what stayed the same.

EQLogParser-Dalaya is a fork of kauffman12's EQLogParser. The vast majority of the application is identical. This page describes what was changed, why it had to change, and what is exclusive to this fork.

What is unchanged

All of the following work exactly as they do in the upstream project:

  • The entire user interface — all windows, tabs, controls, and layout
  • Audio triggers: the Trigger Manager, all trigger variable types, timers, overlays
  • Game overlays — damage meter, countdown timer bars
  • Healing, Tanking, and Spell Counts analysis
  • Deaths, Loot, and Special stats tracking
  • The auto-updater
  • Log backup utility

If you are already familiar with the upstream EQLogParser, everything you know carries over.

Log format differences

Shards of Dalaya runs on EverQuest Emulator (EQEmu), which produces a combat log that differs from Live and TLP EverQuest in several ways. These differences required changes to the damage line parser.

Spell damage attribution

On Live EQ, a caster's spell damage appears as:

Target has taken 500 damage from Caster by SpellName.

On Dalaya, the attacker and spell name are reversed:

Target has taken 500 damage from SpellName by Caster.

This fork detects the Dalaya format and swaps the fields back before processing, so DPS is correctly attributed to the caster rather than the spell name.

Self-damage lines

On Live EQ, your own spells appear with your character name in the damage line. On Dalaya, the word "your" is used instead:

Target has taken 500 damage from your SpellName.

This fork resolves "your" to your character's name (read from the log filename) so your own damage is correctly attributed to you rather than treated as anonymous.

Named pets

Dalaya has named pets that appear in the log with a trailing space in their name due to how EQEmu formats certain log lines. This fork detects the trailing space as the pet identity signal and correctly normalizes the name across melee, spell, and DoT lines without misattributing the pet's kills in raid logs where other players' pets may also appear.

Direct-damage spell attribution

Dalaya's non-melee direct-damage lines carry no spell name at all: 

Target hit by Caster for 500 points of non-melee damage.

This fork tracks each attacker's recent spell casts and, when one of these hits arrives within the expected timing window of a cast, stamps that spell's name onto the hit so it shows up correctly in spell-count and DPS breakdowns instead of as "Unknown".

Spell database

The upstream EQLogParser ships with a spell list built from Live EverQuest data. This fork replaces that entirely with a spells.txt built from Dalaya's actual game files, giving you:

  • Correct Dalaya spell names in DPS and spell count breakdowns
  • Accurate HoT and DoT tick values for Dalaya spells
  • Spell icons matching what you see in game
  • Spell descriptions and SPA effect text from Dalaya's database

Features added in this fork

These features do not exist in the upstream project:

  • Raid Damage multi-log merging — combine log files from multiple players into a single raid-wide DPS and healing view, with automatic clock-offset correction and cross-source deduplication
  • Spell Browser — browse and search the Dalaya spell database with icons, descriptions, and decoded effect text for every spell slot
  • HoT Effectiveness table — per-spell breakdown of HoT tick values received vs. expected, accessible from the Healing Summary right-click menu
  • Spell Details popup — shows all SPA effect slots for any spell in a human-readable format, accessible from the Damage, Healing, Tanking, Spell Counts, and Spell Cast breakdown views
  • Trigger spell picker — browse Dalaya spells when writing trigger patterns and optionally generate complete timer bundles for buff spells

Reporting Dalaya-specific issues

If a log line is parsing incorrectly — wrong player credited, a spell name showing where a player name should be, or a pet appearing as an unknown entity — please open an issue:

github.com/SkepticalMystic/EQLogParser-Dalaya/issues

Include the raw line from your log file. Log files are at:

[EQ folder]\Logs\eqlog_CharacterName_dalaya.txt

For general EQLogParser questions not specific to Dalaya, the upstream GitHub Discussions page is also a good resource.
