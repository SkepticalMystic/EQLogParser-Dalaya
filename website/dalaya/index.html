Real-time combat parser for the Shards of Dalaya EverQuest server

 

Download Latest All Releases

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) • .NET 8 Desktop Runtime required

Features

Damage Parsing

Real-time DPS tracking per player, pet, and spell with full breakdowns of damage dealt and received across any encounter.

Healing Analytics

Track heals, HoTs, and effective healing per caster with per-spell breakdowns and HoT tick effectiveness analysis.

Spell Timelines

Visualize spell cast timelines and ADPS buff coverage for up to two players side-by-side on a shared time axis.

Audio Triggers

Create text-to-speech alerts, sound cues, and timer overlays triggered by mob abilities, spell procs, or any log text.

Game Overlays

Live damage meters, countdown timer bars, and visual feedback displayed directly over the EverQuest window.

Multi-Log Raid View

Merge logs from multiple group members to build a complete raid-wide damage and healing picture in one window.

Built for Dalaya

Shards of Dalaya has a combat log format that differs from Live and TLP EverQuest servers. This fork handles those differences automatically so your parses are always accurate.

  • Spell damage attribution — correctly parses Dalaya's reversed attacker/spell order and your-style self-damage lines
  • Named pet tracking — identifies Dalaya named pets (Bonaparte, Fireballs, Otto, Weens) correctly across melee, spell, and DoT lines without misattribution in raid logs
  • Dalaya spell database — ships with a spells.txt built from Dalaya's actual game data, including spell icons, effects, and descriptions
